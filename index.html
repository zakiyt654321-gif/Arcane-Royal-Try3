📜 ARCANE COMBAT GUIDE

Move & steerSlide your thumb on the left joystick to move and steer.
Aim & cancel castHold and drag any attack button to aim, then release. Drag onto the red ✕ CANCEL zone to abort without spending MP or cooldown.
Dash & Jump PadsTap DASH to burst forward with brief invulnerability, or step onto glowing cyan Arcane Jump Pads on island ridges to launch airborne.
Elemental ReactionsCombine Fire, Water, and Dark abilities within 4s to trigger Vaporize, Shadowflame, and Abyssal Undertow combos.
Quick-Use & inventory fanTap Quick-Use (auto-prioritizes Heal <70% HP or Mana <40% MP) or tap ▲ to fan out all 5 loot items.
Sprint & Bounty CrownMove for 1.5s to auto-sprint free, or hold SPRINT. Fighters with 3+ elims wear a golden 👑 crown worth 2x Coins & +75 XP.
Minimap & quick-pingsDouble-tap the minimap to quick-ping · Tap Map then tap the island to pin · Tap × on the waypoint bar to clear.
Survive & adaptStay inside the safe zone, watch biome weather, and spend XP between matches on 25-tier permanent upgrades.
